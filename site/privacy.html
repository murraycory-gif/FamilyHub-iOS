Privacy policy

HUB Circle is a family board for parents and guardians. It is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian enters a child’s information. Kids do not create their own accounts.

What stays on the device

The household file on this iPhone or iPad can include profiles, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, chores, allowance, calendars, meals, flights, packages, school documents, medical documents, and custody notes. Camera photos and photo-library pictures used for profiles or recipe scans stay on the device. HUB does not upload them to a server we run.

iCloud

The household is saved in your private iCloud zone. People you invite with Apple’s share sheet can see the full household, including the items listed above. The join code is also kept in iCloud key-value storage so this device can find the house again. We cannot read that private zone or that key-value store.

Calendars

HUB reads calendars on this device to fill the family board. If you paste a school or other ICS link, HUB fetches that file from the host you chose. We do not receive a copy.

Location

Location is used only while HUB is open. It is sent to Apple WeatherKit for local weather, and to Apple MapKit and Look Around for nearby places and place pictures. HUB does not track location in the background.

What we do not do

No analytics. No tracking. No advertising. No developer server. Place pictures are not downloaded from other websites.

Erase and Leave

Erase, for the owner, deletes the private iCloud zone and the share, then removes the household from this device. Leave, for someone who was invited, removes this device from the share and clears the local copy. It does not delete the owner’s zone or the owner’s share.
